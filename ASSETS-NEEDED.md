in it (placeholder / repeated asset), and exactly what we need from the
client. General rules for all assets:

- **Videos:** MP4 (H.264), 16:9 landscape, no audio needed (they play muted), 8–15 seconds, ideally under 10 MB each.
- **Product cutouts:** PNG with transparent background, shot straight-on.
